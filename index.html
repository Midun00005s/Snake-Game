PC Controls

Use Arrow keys or W, A, S, D to steer. Press Space or P to pause. Press R to restart.

📱

Mobile Controls

Swipe in any direction anywhere on the screen to steer the snake with smooth gesture control.

🌀

Safe Borders (Zero Wall Death)

Walls never kill you! You safely pass through to the opposite side. The only way to die is biting your own body.
